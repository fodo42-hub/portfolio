Digital Marketing

Multi-channel marketing across social media, email, print, tradeshow, and brand campaigns — built and managed as the sole creative resource.

Overview

One person. Every channel. Every campaign.

As UEi's sole creative lead, I owned the design and production of every digital and print marketing asset the company produced — from weekly email newsletters to tradeshow banners to social media content across four platforms. There was no marketing team. Every campaign, every post, every banner, every ad was conceived, designed, and delivered by me.

The scope covered organic social media management, a weekly distributor newsletter running for 7+ years, tradeshow and print collateral, catalog and publication ads, web banners, Amazon product content, and a branded environmental installation — all maintained in parallel while supporting a 200+ SKU product line.

Channel 01
Social Media
Instagram · Facebook · LinkedIn · X
300% follower growth

I managed UEi's organic social media presence across four platforms — creating all content in-house without an agency or paid advertising budget. Content spanned product launches, seasonal campaigns, giveaways, tradeshow coverage, customer spotlights, and brand awareness posts, all designed to stay consistent with the UEi brand system while adapting tone and format for each platform's audience.

Instagram grew from 1,500 followers when I started to 6,200 — a 300% increase driven entirely by organic content. Each post was designed to be visually on-brand, immediately recognizable as UEi, and relevant to the HVAC/R and electrical contractor audience we were reaching.

Social media content grid — product launches, Halloween campaign, giveaway, USA 250th commemorative edition, and new product reveals

Channel 02
Tradeshow + Print Banners
Standing banners · Distributor events · Tradeshow displays
Physical brand presence

Tradeshow and distributor event materials required designs that worked at large format — bold enough to read across a crowded floor, on-brand enough to be immediately recognizable as UEi. I designed standing banners, display graphics, and event collateral for use at trade events and distributor sales meetings across the US.

Each piece was designed to reinforce the brand campaign message while highlighting the specific product or promotion relevant to that event — from the CO Awareness Month campaign to the USA 250th Commemorative Edition clamp meter launch.

Standing banners — CO Protection "Always On" campaign (left) and USA 250th Commemorative Edition clamp meter launch (right)

Channel 03
Catalog + Publication Ads
Trade publication ads · Distributor catalogs · Product launch spreads
Print + digital

Publication and catalog ads required a different level of polish — these placements ran in trade publications read by HVAC/R professionals, contractors, and distributor sales reps. Every ad had to communicate product value quickly, look premium on the page, and be print-ready at exact publication specs.

The Scales catalog ad shown below — "The Rumors Are True" — is a full-page product launch spread for UEi's wireless refrigerant scale line, combining dramatic product photography, bold headline writing, and a complete product comparison across three SKUs with QR-linked video content.

Full-page catalog ad — UEi Wireless Refrigerant Scales product launch. "The Rumors Are True." Trade publication and distributor catalog placement.

Channel 04
Brand Campaign — Environmental Design
Test · Measure · Analyze · Conference room installation
Brand campaign

The UEi brand campaign extended beyond digital and print into the physical environment. I designed a modular wall installation for UEi's conference room — a grid of 12"×12" and 25"×25" printed panels spanning floor to ceiling (8'6") that brought the company's core brand message, Test · Measure · Analyze, to life as an immersive visual experience.

The installation combined product photography, in-use action shots, brand typography, and milestone moments — including the 50th Anniversary mark — into a cohesive environmental design that reinforced the brand for every visitor, partner, and employee who walked into that room.

Brand campaign wall installation — Test · Measure · Analyze. Conference Room #2. 8'6" floor to ceiling. Modular panel grid with layout specification.

Channel 05
Email Newsletter
Weekly distributor newsletter · Constant Contact · 400+ editions
7+ years · Steady growth

UEi's weekly distributor newsletter has been running for over 7 years — and reached its 400th edition in November 2024. I designed the newsletter template that has carried the publication through hundreds of editions, keeping it visually consistent with the UEi brand system while accommodating a wide range of weekly content: product announcements, podcast links, new product previews, distributor spotlights, industry awareness campaigns, and field updates from the UEi sales team.

The newsletter goes out every Friday morning to UEi's distributor network — the frontline reps and sales teams who represent UEi products to contractors and end users across the US and Canada. Consistent design, reliable delivery, and steady open rate growth over 7+ years reflects the trust the distributor network has placed in this weekly touchpoint.

400th edition — UEi weekly distributor newsletter, November 22, 2024. Template designed and maintained across 7+ years of weekly publication.
